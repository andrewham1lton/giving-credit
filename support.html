Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us. If something isn’t working, or you have an idea, we’d love to hear from you.

Contact us

Email: eighthdayapps@outlook.com

We reply within one business day, usually sooner. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please never send card details, passwords, or your Social Security number, and don’t attach receipts unless we ask.

Is this tax advice?

No. Giving Credit keeps a record of your gifts and the values you choose, and adds them up. You and your tax preparer decide what to deduct.

Where do the suggested values come from?

From donation value guides that charities publish, such as the Salvation Army’s and Goodwill’s. Each range shows the guides it comes from. A suggestion stays in pencil and isn’t counted until you confirm a value.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your records are stored on your iPhone and come back when you restore a backup of it to a new iPhone. There is no account or online copy, so keep your iPhone backed up.

Subscriptions and refunds

Cancel: in Giving Credit, go to Settings, then Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Free trial: the yearly plan has a 7-day free trial, and if you allow notifications we remind you 2 days before it ends.

Subscription missing: in Giving Credit, go to Settings, then Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

If your subscription ends: everything you recorded stays on your iPhone and you can still read it. Adding or changing gifts needs a subscription again.

Refunds: purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, choose “I’d like to”, then “Request a refund”.

Privacy

Giving Credit collects no data: no account, no server, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Read our privacy policy.
