An ItsDeductible alternative for iPhone

Intuit shut down ItsDeductible in October 2025. If you used it to keep track of the bags you dropped at the thrift store and the checks you wrote to charity, Giving Credit is an iPhone app built for the same job: record each donation, put a value on donated goods, and have one summary ready at tax time.

Giving Credit is an independent app, made by one developer. It isn’t made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Intuit.

Giving Credit: Donation Tracker, for iPhone (iOS 26 or later).

Coming to the App Store

Record your first gift free. After that it’s an annual subscription with a free trial; the price and trial length are shown in the app before you confirm.

What it does

  • Donated goods. Photograph the drop-off and list what was in it. A built-in guide of nearly 200 common items shows an estimate range for each, the charity guides the range comes from and when they were checked.
  • You confirm every value. A suggested value stays in pencil and isn’t counted in any total until you confirm it.
  • Money gifts. Add a gift by amount, charity, date and how you paid, or photograph the receipt and Giving Credit reads it on your iPhone and offers the amount, date and charity for you to check.
  • Proof. Every gift shows its proof status. Gifts of $250 or more show “letter needed” until you have the charity’s letter, and Giving Credit drafts the request for you to send from your own Mail.
  • Charity lookup. Search the IRS list of organizations (Publication 78 data, dated in the app) by name and city to fill in a charity’s name, EIN and address.
  • Volunteer miles, recorded at the charity rate of 14 cents a mile.
  • Larger items. Items over $500 ask for the details Form 8283 asks for; items over $5,000 are flagged as needing a qualified appraisal.

Bring your ItsDeductible records with you

If you exported your data before ItsDeductible closed, Giving Credit can import the Items, Money and Mileage CSV files (or the ZIP that holds them): in the app, go to Settings, then Import from ItsDeductible.

  • The files are read on your iPhone and aren’t sent anywhere.
  • You see a preview before anything is added. Item values arrive in pencil until you confirm them, and each money gift and trip is added only if you switch it on.
  • Importing the same files twice doesn’t add anything twice.
  • Stock donations aren’t imported; Giving Credit records money, goods and mileage.
  • Import is part of the subscription, and the free trial includes it.

Intuit never published the layout of those files, so if one of yours doesn’t read, the preview says which file and which line. Email us and we’ll look into it: eighthdayapps@outlook.com. Please don’t send the file itself unless we ask.

Not sure where your files are? See getting your records out of ItsDeductible.

What you get at tax time

  • A PDF summary of the year: every gift and donation, its proof status, your totals and a Form 8283 Section A table.
  • A CSV with one row per gift, item and mileage entry.
  • A proof bundle of the year’s receipts, letters and item photos.
  • The IRS Form 8283 with your name and Section A filled in from your confirmed goods. It stays editable for you or your preparer to finish.
  • A .txf file of your confirmed totals for TurboTax Desktop’s File › Import.

These leave your iPhone only when you share them yourself.

How it differs from ItsDeductible

  • iPhone only. There is no website, iPad, Mac or Android version.
  • No account and no online copy. Your records are stored on your iPhone and come back when you restore a backup of it. Nothing is sent to us.
  • No link to TurboTax Online. ItsDeductible sent your totals into TurboTax. Giving Credit makes files instead: the summary for you or your preparer, and a .txf file that TurboTax Desktop can import.
  • It’s paid. ItsDeductible was free. Giving Credit is an annual subscription after your first gift, with a free trial. If your subscription ends, everything you recorded stays on your iPhone to read and export.
  • Its own value guide. The ranges come from donation value guides that charities publish, such as the Salvation Army’s and Goodwill’s, not from ItsDeductible’s figures. They are estimates, not appraisals.

Is this tax advice?

No. Giving Credit keeps a record of your gifts and the values you choose, and adds them up. It never decides what you can deduct: you and your tax preparer do.
