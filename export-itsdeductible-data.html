Getting your records out of ItsDeductible

Intuit closed ItsDeductible on October 21, 2025. If you used it, your records may be in one of two places: files you downloaded before it closed, or a data download from your Intuit Account that you can still request.

Files you exported before the shutdown

ItsDeductible’s own export covered tax years 2021 to 2025, as a ZIP or as separate CSV files, one per kind of gift:

  • Items: charity, address, date, description, quality, quantity and value.
  • Money: charity, address, date, payment type and amount.
  • Mileage: charity, address, date, miles and value.
  • Stock: charity, date, symbol, cost and value.

Look in your Downloads folder or email for a ZIP or for files with these names. The columns above are as reported by other importers; Intuit never published the layout. The files have no charity EIN, only a name and one-line address.

A data download from your Intuit Account

If you didn’t export in time, Intuit’s help pages say your Intuit Account’s data download includes ItsDeductible data:

  1. Sign in to your Intuit Account and open Data privacy.
  2. Choose Download and request your data. Intuit says a request can take up to 15 days.
  3. In the download, the ItsDeductible data is inside the SharedData folder, reported as an Excel file named ItsDeductibleOnline.xlsx.

The Excel file holds the same kinds of records but, as reported, with different column names from the CSV export.

What not to expect

  • TurboTax doesn’t import ItsDeductible files. Intuit’s replies point to typing your donations in.
  • A PDF summary is a printout, not data; keep it as a record.
  • Keep the original files even after you move your records somewhere else. They are your record of what you entered at the time.

Moving to another tool? See Giving Credit as an ItsDeductible alternative.

Where Giving Credit fits

Giving Credit imports the Items, Money and Mileage CSV files from the pre-shutdown export, or the ZIP that holds them. The files are read on your iPhone, you see a preview first, and item values arrive in pencil until you confirm them. It doesn’t read the Excel file from the data download.
