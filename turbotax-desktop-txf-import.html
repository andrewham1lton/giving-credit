Importing donations into TurboTax Desktop with a TXF file

TXF (Tax Exchange Format) is a plain-text file that desktop tax software can import. Instead of typing your donation totals into TurboTax Desktop, you import a .txf file and the totals land in the right lines.

Importing it

  1. Open your return in TurboTax Desktop, on Mac or Windows.
  2. Choose File › Import, and pick the .txf file.
  3. Check the imported figures against your own summary before you go on.

What a donations TXF fills in

  • Gifts by cash or check on Schedule A: money gifts, and mileage at 14 cents a mile.
  • Gifts other than by cash or check on Schedule A: donated goods.

These are itemized deductions. They matter only if you itemize; ask your preparer if you’re unsure.

What you still finish in TurboTax

  • Form 8283 for goods over $500: each item’s charity, dates, how you acquired it and its cost. See what Section A asks for.
  • Items over $5,000 need a qualified appraisal and Form 8283 Section B.
  • Proof isn’t imported. Keep your receipts and letters yourself.

Don’t count gifts twice

After importing, don’t enter the same gifts again in TurboTax’s donation questions, or they’ll count twice. To start over, choose File › Remove Imported Data and import again.

Where Giving Credit fits

Giving Credit makes a .txf file of your confirmed totals for TurboTax Desktop, named for the tax year, from its year-end summary. Values in pencil are left out. Making the file is part of the subscription.
