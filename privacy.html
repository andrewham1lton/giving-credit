Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Giving Credit doesn’t collect any of your data. There’s no account, no server, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or SDKs. Your gifts, photos and values stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Everything you record is stored only on your iPhone: the charities, the gifts and donated items, the gifts you plan, the amounts and values you confirm, notes, any photos of receipts, letters or items you add, the filing status and deduction you choose in Settings, the name you put on your year-end summary, and the day you last asked each charity for an acknowledgment letter from Mail. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Like the rest of your device, it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, depending on your settings, so it comes back when you restore or move to a new iPhone. Giving Credit doesn’t use iCloud sync.

Photos and receipts

Photos are kept with the gift as your record. Any reading of a photo, such as finding the amount on a receipt, happens on your iPhone, not by an online service.

Files you share in or import

When you share a receipt or PDF to Giving Credit from Mail, Files or another app, it is copied into a private folder on your iPhone that only Giving Credit can read, read there, and removed once it is used (or after 7 days if it never is). When you import a CSV file you exported from another app, such as ItsDeductible, it is read on your iPhone and not kept. Nothing you share in or import is sent anywhere.

Widget and Siri

The Home Screen widget shows your year’s total and how many things need your attention, from a small file Giving Credit writes on your iPhone for it; the figures are hidden while your iPhone is locked. Asking Siri or Shortcuts to log a donation opens Giving Credit on your iPhone; Giving Credit sends nothing to Apple or anyone else.

Value estimates

The suggested value ranges come from a guide built into the app, compiled from donation value guides that charities publish. Looking up a value never goes online.

Sharing your summary

Your records leave your iPhone only when you share them yourself, for example sending the year-end summary to your tax preparer. You choose who receives it, and we never do.

When you ask a charity for an acknowledgment letter, Giving Credit drafts the email and opens it in your own Mail app, or copies it for you to paste. You choose the address and send it yourself; Giving Credit never sends anything.

Purchases

The Giving Credit subscription is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple gives developers anonymous sales totals.

Reminders

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the reminder before your trial ends is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. If you plan gifts and allow notifications, each plan’s next date is a local notification too; it says only that a planned gift is due, never the charity or the amount, because it can show on your lock screen. We don’t use push notifications and never receive a device token.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and diagnostic reports. They help fix bugs and don’t identify you. We receive these only through Apple. You control this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

How long we keep things

  • Everything on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Who else is involved

Apple handles payments and, only if you opt in, crash reports, under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Children

Giving Credit is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
