Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Giving Credit doesn’t collect any of your data. There’s no account, no server, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or SDKs. Your gifts, photos and values stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Everything you record is stored only on your iPhone: the charities, the gifts and donated items, the amounts and values you confirm, notes, and any photos of receipts, letters or items you add. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Like the rest of your device, it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, depending on your settings, so it comes back when you restore or move to a new iPhone. Giving Credit doesn’t use iCloud sync.

Photos and receipts

Photos are kept with the gift as your record. Any reading of a photo, such as finding the amount on a receipt, happens on your iPhone, not by an online service.

Value estimates

The suggested value ranges come from a guide built into the app, compiled from donation value guides that charities publish. Looking up a value never goes online.

Sharing your summary

Your records leave your iPhone only when you share them yourself, for example sending the year-end summary to your tax preparer. You choose who receives it, and we never do.

Purchases

The Giving Credit subscription is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple gives developers anonymous sales totals.

Reminders

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the reminder before your trial ends is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. We don’t use push notifications and never receive a device token.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and diagnostic reports. They help fix bugs and don’t identify you. We receive these only through Apple. You control this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

How long we keep things

  • Everything on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Who else is involved

Apple handles payments and, only if you opt in, crash reports, under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Children

Giving Credit is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email eighthdayapps@outlook.com. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.
